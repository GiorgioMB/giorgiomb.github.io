Postulates

Postulate IError bars are as important as the quantity they come with; without them, estimation is alchemy with a new name.

Postulate IIData are samples from a manifold, and what is true lives on the manifold rather than in any one sample.

Conjectures

Conjecture IA graph has a natural tangent space at each vertex and each edge, and a curvature operator on it that plays the part Ricci curvature plays on a manifold.

Status. Consistency and convergence are shown; optimality is not, yet.

The tangent space is spanned by increments along the half-edges, and the operator is the curvature term of a discrete Bochner formula. At a vertex it recovers the curvature matrix of Cushing, Kamtue, Liu and Peyerimhoff (2022).

Conjecture IIBootstrap error bars for deep networks measure the wrong thing. The bootstrap treats each network retrained on a resample as a fresh draw of one estimator, but each retraining settles in a different minimum, so the spread mixes the noise in the data with the randomness of training. Methods built the same way inherit the problem.

Status. Open.

Errata

Erratum II used to think that if data lie on a manifold, geometric methods are the natural way to study them. I still think they lie on one, but rarely one tame enough to use. Curvature is the clearest case: estimated from a point cloud it is noisy, and the sample has to be far denser than real data usually are before the estimate means anything.

Some notions only work once they are heavily modified: Ollivier curvature of random geometric graphs converges to Ricci curvature only after it is generalised to mesoscopic neighbourhoods (van der Hoorn and others, 2020).

Erratum III thought Ollivier–Ricci curvature would be easy to bound by Balanced Forman curvature, since a cruder rule already holds: on an edge whose endpoints have degrees di and dj and which lies on T triangles, it sits between −2 + 3T/max(di, dj) and T/max(di, dj), a loosened form of the bound of Jost and Liu. Balanced Forman also counts the 4-cycles through the edge, and turning it into a two-sided bound took a paper.

What came of it is Proposition I.
